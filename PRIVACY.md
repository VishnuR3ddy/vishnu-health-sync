# Privacy Policy — Vishnu Health Sync

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Vishnu Health Sync is a personal automation, not a public service. It exists
solely to let me (the developer) read my own Google Health data.

**Data collected:** Google Health activity/fitness, sleep, and health-metric
data from my own Google account, via read-only OAuth scopes.

**How it's used:** Pulled by a script running on my own machine into my
personal Google Sheet. No other use.

**Sharing:** None. Data is never sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

**Storage:** Local script config and my personal Google Sheet only.
